--- title: Pureley Functional date: 1970-01-01 # used for the page order (chronologically) learn_more: https://www-ps.informatik.uni-kiel.de/currywiki/ color: blue --- Curry is called a pure language, because functions in Curry are functions in the mathematical sense. Operations with side-effects are explictly marked as "IO" operations, and thus, a description of what to do, produced by pure code. Functions are constructed by expressions only, there are no statements or instructions, and every bindings to variables are immutable.
