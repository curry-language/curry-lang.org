--- title: Free Variables date: 1970-01-04 # used for the page order (chronologically) learn_more: https://www-ps.informatik.uni-kiel.de/currywiki/ --- Free variables denote “unknown” values. They are instantiated (i.e., replaced by some concrete values) so that the instantiated expression is evaluable. REPls of Curry systems show bindings (i.e., instantiations) of the free variables it has compute to evalute an expression.
