--- title: Purely Declarative date: 1970-01-01 # used for the page order (chronologically) learn_more: https://www-ps.informatik.uni-kiel.de/currywiki/ --- Curry is called a declarative language, because computed results are independent of the time and order of evaluation, which simplifies reasoning on programs. Side effects can be modeled as "IO" operations, i.e., a declarative description of what to do. Operations are constructed by expressions only, there are no statements or instructions, and every binding to a variable is immutable.
